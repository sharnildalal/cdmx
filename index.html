Morning

Panadería Rosetta

Bakery Roma Norte

Best bakery in the city. Pastries, coffee, ease into the day. Walking distance from the Airbnb.

12:30 PM

Xochimilco Boat Tour

Activity Xochimilco Cash Only Reserved ✓

Hire a trajinera (~750 pesos/hr for the whole boat). 2 hours on the ancient canals, bring your own beer and snacks. Weekends are busier with floating mariachis and food vendors.

Getting there: ~45 min Uber from Roma Norte. Arrive by 12:15 PM. Leave the Airbnb by 11:30.
Afternoon

Back to Airbnb - Rest Up

Roma Norte

Recharge before the night crawl. You're going to need it.

Sunset

Supra Roma

Rooftop Roma Norte

Art meets food and drinks - the best sunset views in the neighborhood. Good music, great way to start the evening.

Dinner

Expendio de Maíz

Restaurant Roma Norte Cash Only No Set Menu

Put your name down early - wait can be 2 hours. No menu: you tell them what you eat, they cook for you. Incredible vegetarian options too. One of the most unique dining experiences in the city.

Bring cash. They're strict about it. No cards accepted.
Night

Handshake Speakeasy

Bar Centro #2 North America · #11 World

1920s vibe, unique drinks, great hospitality. Reservations recommended. The bar that put CDMX on the world cocktail map.

Hanky Panky

Another world-ranked speakeasy. Staff can be a bit cool, but the drinks are worth it.

Patrick Miller

Pure dancing. No frills, no pretension - just a dance floor and energy. The perfect closer.

Cultura + Pujol

Morning

Centro Histórico Walk

Activity Centro Histórico

Palacio de Bellas Artes - the Art Nouveau/Art Deco masterpiece.
Templo Mayor - Aztec ruins right in the heart of the city.

Afternoon

Museo Nacional de Antropología

Activity Chapultepec

One of the greatest museums in the world. The Aztec Sun Stone, Maya exhibits, the massive courtyard fountain - give it at least 2–3 hours. Inside Chapultepec Park.

~4:30 PM

Hidden underground vegan taco stand right at the entrance to Chapultepec. Dirt-cheap, insanely good, 4.6 stars. Try the campechano and tinga de setas. Quick refuel after the museum, then Uber to the Airbnb to change for Pujol.

7:30 PM

Pujol

Jazz and blues bar. Cozy, intimate vibe - the perfect nightcap after Pujol. Mezcal in hand, live music.
